About Our Project

Making community assistance easier to find and understand in Missoula, Montana.

Who We Are

Missoula Community Connect is a student website prototype designed for a nonprofit organization that helps residents discover essential community resources.

Our goal is to create a simple and accessible website where people can explore information about housing, food, healthcare, transportation, and utility assistance.

Our Mission

We believe community resources should be easy to find and understand. Our mission is to reduce barriers by organizing important information in one clear and welcoming place.

Our Design Priorities

Accessibility

Create a website that is easy to navigate and read on different devices.

Clarity

Present information using simple language and clear organization.

Community

Help residents discover resources that may support their everyday needs.

Contact & Information

This website was created as an educational project for a Web Design course. It is not an official assistance service.

For questions about a specific assistance program, please contact the organization providing that service directly.
